# Business

RelayPilot is designed as a commercial independent product, but commercial infrastructure is deliberately outside the current engineering phase.

## Direction

- One product and one cross-platform entitlement.
- Buy the current feature generation once.
- Optional paid access to later feature generations; the purchased version keeps working.
- A lower price than buying Surge separately across its available platforms.
- No required account or server in the initial product.

Sync begins with user-owned storage. Apple devices should prefer iCloud Drive. Local folders and WebDAV provide portable options. A hosted RelayPilot service is a future product decision, not an architectural dependency.

## Evidence before infrastructure

The project should prove, in order:

1. a reliable real proxy path;
2. a consistent profile on at least two materially different platforms;
3. deterministic connection explanations users trust;
4. durable user-owned sync;
5. willingness to pay.

Only then should accounts, license delivery, hosted sync, or metered AI be designed.
