# Product

RelayPilot exists to give one person one understandable network across every device they own.

## Product boundary

Proxying is a foundational capability, not the product boundary. The product is the consistent profile, policy, connection, explanation, diagnosis, and sync experience around that capability.

The first paying audience is technically capable users in mainland China who already maintain several proxy clients because no single product treats macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad, Android, and Apple TV as one system.

## North star

The same intent produces the same observable decision on every device.

For any connection RelayPilot should answer, without an AI model:

1. What destination did the app request?
2. Which DNS policy applied?
3. Which rule matched, from which source and line?
4. Which policy group was requested?
5. Which member did the group select, and why?
6. Which engine executed the outbound?
7. Where did the connection fail or slow down?

## Not now

- accounts, licensing infrastructure, hosted sync, telemetry, or a required RelayPilot service
- protocol invention or a ground-up replacement for mature proxy engines
- MITM, rewrite, scripting, team management, or an AI chat surface
- false platform checkmarks before a native host and real tunnel path exist
